THE GAP

Agent output is easy.
Trustworthy review is not.

01

Chat loses place

Feedback drifts away from the exact sentence and version it describes.

02

Files lose intent

A final export rarely explains what changed, who requested it, or why.

03

Developer tools add friction

Most reviewers should not need branches, diffs, or repository concepts.
