01 / EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLES

The interface should disappear behind the document.

01

Document first

The reading canvas owns the visual hierarchy. Review controls stay quiet until needed.

02

Reader language

Use Comments, Versions, Current, and Previous instead of storage or graph terminology.

03

Explicit outcomes

Draft, post, resolve, update, and publish remain separate, visible owner actions.
